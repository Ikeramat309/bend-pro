# Back-to-Back 90 - competitor comparison

QuickBend and iBend Pipe list back-to-back/90 workflows and emphasize bender-specific data. The manufacturer procedure is clearer than the public competitor documentation for this particular job.

Bend Pro should win here through procedural clarity:

- hero includes the physical origin and Star;
- hook direction is drawn;
- same-plane check is part of the result;
- Tight-U alternate is available without contaminating the default;
- first stub deduct appears only when requested.

Do not add extra values simply to resemble a dense competitor screen. This job is strongest when it remains nearly appliance-simple.
