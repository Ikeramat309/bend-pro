# Back-to-Back 90 - diagram brief

## Hero state

- Steel EMT in an elongated U.
- The physical back plane of Bend 1 is drawn and labeled `Start here`.
- One dimension runs from that plane to a narrow wrap mark for Bend 2.
- `2 - Star` badge is attached to the wrap.
- Hook arrow sits at Bend 2 and points toward the free end.
- A subtle plane rail under both legs communicates `keep flat`.

If the optional first stub is enabled, add `1 - Arrow` without competing with the second-mark hero.

## Tight-U state

Use a visibly different bend-in-progress pose. Change the Bend 2 badge to Arrow and reverse the hook cue toward Bend 1. Add a compact caution that the formed first bend moves toward the operator. Never reuse the standard diagram with only the text changed.

## Collision rules

- Keep the distance label outside the U interior at narrow dimensions.
- Use leader lines from result badges to marks.
- At very long distances compress the straight segment with a break cue; never scale mark wraps or pipe OD into invisibility.
- Keep endpoint cut faces clean and consistent with other steel-tube diagrams.

## Test cases

Short/long distance, optional stub, Tight-U, large unit text, smallest phone, light/dark, and bender-reference warning.
