# Back-to-Back 90 - field workflow

## Starting state

The electrician knows the finished distance between two parallel surfaces. Bend 1 may already exist or may be laid out as an optional stub.

## Direct Star method

1. Measure the distance between the surfaces where the backs of the 90s must land.
2. Make Bend 1 if needed.
3. Place the formed 90 flat so its back is identifiable.
4. Measure from the back edge along the straight conduit and mark the full finished distance.
5. Place the bender with hook facing the free end, opposite Bend 1.
6. Align the mark to Star.
7. Bend to 90 while keeping Bend 1 in the same plane.
8. Check the U against the surfaces before cutting a free leg.

## Tight-U Arrow alternate

Use only when the bender cannot physically fit the direct Star setup. Enter the first stub height. Follow the manufacturer-specific alternate diagram: the mark calculation, Arrow reference, and hook-facing-first-bend instruction replace the standard second-bend instructions.

## Common failures

- Measuring from the inside tangent or center of Bend 1 instead of its back.
- Aligning the second mark to Arrow in standard mode.
- Leaving the hook in the same orientation as Bend 1.
- Lifting/twisting the first leg and creating a dog-leg.
- Trying the tight method without enough remaining straight conduit.

## Check

The conduit should lie flat, both legs should be parallel, and the outside backs should match the measured surface distance.
