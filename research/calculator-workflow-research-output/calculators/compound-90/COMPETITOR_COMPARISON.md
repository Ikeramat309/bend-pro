# Compound 90 - competitor comparison

QuickBend and iBend Pipe list separate round, rectangle, and square Compound 90 workflows. That validates discoverability by obstruction, but the public listings do not prove which square orientation their labels mean.

Bend Pro should improve the category by:

- using pictured orientation rather than relying on `square`;
- showing center-to-center marks as the hero;
- making half-OD conversion and clearance traceable in Guide;
- keeping the 45-degree center reference connected to the selected bender;
- stating that the workflow solves the compound bend spacing, not all final leg lengths.

Do not infer competitor CLR behavior from marketing or copy its formulas without a reproducible on-device case.
