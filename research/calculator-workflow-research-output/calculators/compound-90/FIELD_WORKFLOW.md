# Compound 90 - field workflow

## Starting state

Straight EMT approaches a corner where a round or box-shaped obstruction prevents a normal 90. The user intends to make a 90-degree direction change using two 45s.

## What the electrician can measure

- Round: diameter.
- Wall-aligned box: vertical height and horizontal width of the envelope at the corner.
- Diamond: side length of the turned square shown by the selector.
- Optional: extra clearance desired between the obstacle envelope and back of conduit.
- Optional: the first bend-center station from a free conduit end.

## Sequence

1. Match the on-screen silhouette to the real obstruction.
2. Measure only the dimensions highlighted on that silhouette.
3. Enter EMT size so the app can convert the source's back-of-conduit distance to center-to-center marks.
4. Mark Center 1 and Center 2.
5. Use the selected bender's mapped 45-degree center reference. Do not substitute Star; Star means back of a 90.
6. Bend both 45s in one plane, in the displayed order.
7. Hold the piece to the corner and check clearance before cutting the finished legs.

## Common failures

- Choosing diamond because the obstacle is square even though its sides are parallel to the walls.
- Measuring only one side of a wall-aligned rectangle.
- Treating back-of-conduit distance as mark spacing and missing the half-OD conversion.
- Aligning center marks to an Arrow or Star without a reference conversion.
- Adding clearance mentally in only one dimension.
- Assuming the returned spacing also determines final leg length.

## Bend order and plane

The order may be reversed when the physical stick/bender fit demands it, but both marks remain bend centers and both bends remain 45 degrees. The result diagram numbers the default sequence and shows a same-plane rail.
