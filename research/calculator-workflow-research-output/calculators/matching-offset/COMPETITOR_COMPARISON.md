# Matching Offset - competitor comparison

QuickBend separates Matching Centers Offset and Matching Bends Offset, provides center-finding documentation, and includes a protractor. That validates the job and the need for center/angle tooling.

Bend Pro should consolidate the two measurements behind illustrated cards while improving actionability:

- natural measurement names;
- exact angle and center spacing as one hero;
- explicit executable/uncommon angle state;
- no silent snap;
- transparent common-angle comparison;
- center-finding and shoe/CLR limitations in Guide.

The goal is not fewer menu entries by itself. The goal is one mental model with no ambiguity about which length was measured.
