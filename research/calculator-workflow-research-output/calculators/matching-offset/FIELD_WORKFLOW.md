# Matching Offset - field workflow

## Starting state

One offset is already installed or bent. A new straight stick must match its rise, centers, direction, and final parallel run.

## Find the existing bend centers

Preferred when known: use verified original center marks.

Traditional reconstruction:

1. Lay the reference offset flat.
2. Hold a straightedge along the straight conduit before a bend and extend its line through the bend.
3. Repeat along the straight section after the bend.
4. Their intersection locates the bend center in the plane of the offset.
5. Repeat for the second bend and transfer center stations to the conduit centerline measurement.

This competitor-documented method belongs in Guide and must be illustrated; it is not a one-line tooltip.

## Measure

- Rise is perpendicular distance between the two parallel straight legs.
- Along the Run is the projection between bend centers in the original run direction.
- Along the Pipe is center-to-center distance following the sloped straight segment.

## Bend the match

1. Mark two centers at the returned spacing.
2. Confirm whether the exact angle has a supported bender cue.
3. Bend Center 1.
4. Rotate the conduit exactly 180 degrees in the same plane.
5. Bend Center 2 to the same angle in the opposite direction.
6. Compare center stations, rise, and parallel legs to the original.

## Common failures

- Measuring from tangent/edge locations instead of centers.
- Selecting Along the Run but measuring along the sloped pipe.
- Treating a 14.0-degree result as `close enough to 15` without seeing the difference.
- Reproducing the angle but using a different center reference on Bend 2.
- Introducing a dog-leg during the 180-degree rotation.
