# Parallel Kick 90s - competitor comparison

QuickBend publicly lists `Parallel Kick` and `Parallel Kick: Forward`, proving that at least two interaction families are valuable. Public listings do not define their exact pictures or input semantics, and the local UI pack is unavailable in this session.

Direct reviews and public threads show why this workflow matters:

- experienced electricians name parallel kicks among the complex jobs for which they use apps;
- users repeatedly ask how to preserve rack and landing spacing;
- one review reports a reference-pipe control problem;
- many discussions recommend QuickBend instead of explaining the geometry.

Bend Pro's differentiator should be:

- picture-first geometry selection;
- explicit statement of which spacing is preserved;
- center-of-90 and center-of-kick shown separately;
- per-pipe mark table with stable Pipe 1;
- calibration/reference honesty instead of a generic bender symbol;
- Guide explanation that makes the result teachable.

Do not copy the competitor's `Forward` label without its visual meaning.
