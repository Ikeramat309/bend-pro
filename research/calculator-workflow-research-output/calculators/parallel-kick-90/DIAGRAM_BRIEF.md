# Parallel Kick 90s - diagram brief

## Geometry chooser

Use a top/isometric hybrid with four short conduits and a cabinet plane. The three cards must differ visibly before labels are read:

- rack traveling beside cabinet, fanning into spaced landings;
- rack traveling toward cabinet and turning into it;
- staggered kick centers preserving equal gap through the diagonal.

Highlight Pipe 1 and animate or arrow the direction to Pipe N. No terminology-only segmented control.

## Hero diagram

- Three representative steel EMT pieces even when count is larger; table carries all rows.
- 90 center and kick center use different mark-wrap colors/shapes.
- `from back of 90` dimension terminates at the physical back plane and the kick center.
- Kick vector is attached to Pipe 1.
- Rack C-C and landing C-C are shown on different planes and use different colors.
- Kick angle arc is anchored to the kick.
- Plane/cabinet reference stays behind the pipe but remains visible in light and dark mode.

## Result table linkage

Selecting a row highlights that pipe and its dimension path. Pipe 1 remains fixed. The diagram never renumbers automatically when direction changes; direction change is an explicit `Reverse rack order` action.

## Collision/scale rules

- Render centerlines first, then pipe stroke, then mark wraps, then vectors, then labels.
- Move value labels to perimeter lanes; keep leaders connected.
- Clamp visual fan spread while preserving ordering; add `diagram compressed` when necessary.
- For large counts show first, second, and last representative pipes plus the complete accessible table.

## Required visual cases

Angles 10, 22.5, 30, 45, and 60 degrees; two and ten pipes; small and large OD; left/right kick; large first kick; smallest phone; light/dark.
