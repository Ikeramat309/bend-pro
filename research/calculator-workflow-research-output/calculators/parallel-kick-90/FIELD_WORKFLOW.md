# Parallel Kick 90s - field workflow

## Starting physical state

A rack of straight EMT must make a kick and a 90-degree turn. The electrician knows the rack spacing, desired first kick, approximate kick angle, pipe count, and how the rack approaches the cabinet/wall.

## Before using the calculator

1. Look down the intended path of the rack.
2. Choose the picture matching whether the run travels beside the cabinet or into it.
3. Choose which physical pipe is Pipe 1.
4. Confirm whether the user is preserving rack spacing through the kick or targeting cabinet knockout centers. Those are not automatically the same constraint.

## Mark and bend sequence - fanned/parallel-to-cabinet case

1. Bend or establish the first 90 and identify its back and centerline.
2. From the back of that 90, mark the kick center returned for Pipe 1.
3. Repeat from each pipe's own back of 90 using its row; do not measure Pipe 2 from Pipe 1.
4. Align each center to the mapped center reference, or convert to front-of-shoe with the calibrated shoe factor.
5. Orient every kick in the displayed direction and bend to the same angle.
6. Check the first two pieces together before committing the rack.
7. Verify landing C-C at the cabinet and fit before cutting.

## Reference calibration from the primary training method

If the bender lacks a verified center reference for the selected angle:

1. Bend scrap to that angle.
2. Use straightedges along the two straight legs to locate the bend center.
3. Measure from the front of the shoe to that center; save this as the shoe factor for the bender/size/angle.
4. Subtract the shoe factor from each center-from-back result to get a front-of-shoe station.

## Common failures

- Measuring kick from the back of 90 without adding half EMT OD to reach centerline.
- Using Arrow for a center result.
- Selecting the wrong rack/cabinet picture because the labels sound familiar.
- Applying one pipe's station to every pipe in a fanned rack.
- Expecting rack C-C and cabinet KO C-C to remain equal in a geometry where projection changes them.
- Cutting all legs before checking the first two bends.
