# Parallel Kick 90s - open questions

## Release blockers

1. Validate the three chooser pictures with the founder and at least one electrician; wording alone is insufficient.
2. Map verified center references or shoe factors for supported hand benders and angles.
3. Confirm the derived per-pipe fanned table against a second worked source or controlled geometry test.
4. Confirm exactly which geometry QuickBend calls `Parallel Kick` versus `Parallel Kick: Forward`; public names alone are not enough.
5. Decide whether the first release supports only cabinet modes or also the half-angle through-bend progression.

## Important product questions

1. Should the user enter desired rack C-C, desired landing C-C, or choose which one is fixed and let the app solve the other?
2. Is `first kick` measured at conduit centerline or outside surface in the user's field language? The input illustration must settle this.
3. How should Bend Pro teach the unavoidable change in the non-preserved spacing without overwhelming the Pro flow?
4. Does the rack need a first 90/stub height input, or is center-from-back sufficient for the first release?

## Deferred

- Mixed sizes
- Rolling/3D kick 90s
- Non-90 main bends
- Automatic cut length and coupling alignment
- Powered/hydraulic bender travel methods
