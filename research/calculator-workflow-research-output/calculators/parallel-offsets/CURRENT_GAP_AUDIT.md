# Parallel Offsets - current gap audit

## Correct foundations

- Half-angle tangent progression is supported.
- Quick Shift and Full Layout belong to one mental model.
- Full Layout can reuse normal offset DBB.

## Required refinements

- Pipe 1 must stay visually and semantically fixed.
- Count belongs in Quick Shift if the product promises a full action table.
- Direction must reference a physical conduit end, not only `advance/retard` or a plus/minus sign.
- Every row must show that both marks move together.
- Absolute center marks must state their origin and reference method.
- Selected bender must not imply that the progression formula used shoe CLR.

## User-evidence connection

App reviews request more rack variations and report trouble with a competitor reference-pipe control. Experienced electricians cite parallel offsets as a reason to use an app. A stable reference and complete table are therefore core, not polish.
